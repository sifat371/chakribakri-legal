Privacy Policy

App: চাকরিবাকরি (ChakriBakri) · Package: com.udvabtech.chakribakri
Developer: Md Sifat / Udvab Tech · Effective date: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information the ChakriBakri app handles, where it is kept, and how you can delete it. The app is a job-preparation app for Bangladesh: exam guides, circulars, practice questions, model tests and study plans.

1. Information the app stores on your device

The app stores the following only on your phone, in its own private storage:

  • Profile you enter: name, study goal, division, education level, and date of birth if you enter it.
  • Your preparation data: study-plan tasks, readiness checks, saved questions, practice answers, model-test results, streak, points and history.
  • App settings: theme, sound, haptics, font and notification preferences.
  • If you use the scholarship claim screen: the payment method you pick, the wallet number and account name you type, and the claim status.

In this version, this information is not sent to us or to any third party. The app has no user accounts and no sign-in.

2. Information sent over the internet

The app needs internet access to open links you choose (for example, an official notice on a government website). Opening a link is handled by your browser and is subject to that website's own policy. The app does not send your profile or practice data to any server in this version.

AI features are not active in this version. If they are switched on in a later version, this policy will be updated before that happens, and you will be told what text is sent and where.

3. Advertising, analytics and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs and no tracking.

4. Permissions

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: open web links and check whether you are online.
  • VIBRATE: haptic feedback when you answer a question or finish a test.
  • WAKE_LOCK: keep the screen on during a timed test.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: play short sound effects.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, call logs, photos or files on your device.

5. Backup

Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account, depending on your device settings. You can turn off Android backup in your phone's settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. To delete all app data, either clear the app's storage in your phone's Settings (Apps → চাকরিবাকরি → Storage → Clear storage), or uninstall the app. Either removes all of the data listed in section 1 from your device. We cannot recover it, because we do not hold a copy.

7. Children

The app is intended for job aspirants and adult learners. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps on your device cannot read under normal Android protections. Keep your phone's screen lock enabled.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

10. Contact
